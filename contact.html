Questions, answered

Is there a minimum order?

No strict minimums. Order a small batch for your team or a bulk run for an event. Bulk pricing tiers kick in as quantities go up, so the more you order, the better the price per piece.

Is the mockup really free?

Yes. Send your logo (or upload it in the Merch Studio) and we'll create a custom mockup so you can see exactly how it'll look before anything gets pressed.

Do you ship outside of Reno?

We do. NeeksxArt is based in Reno, NV and ships nationwide. Let us know your city and deadline and we'll confirm shipping options and timing.

How long does an order take?

It depends on the product, quantity and what's in the queue. Tell us your event date in the form and we'll let you know right away whether we can hit it.

How does Tap-to-Connect work?

Each Tap-to-Connect keychain has an NFC chip inside. We program it with your link (Instagram, website, booking page, menu, anything) and any modern smartphone opens it with a tap. No app required.

What file should I send?

A high-resolution PNG with a transparent background is ideal. SVG, PDF, AI or a clear JPG work too. Don't have a clean file? Send what you have and we'll help.
